By using this website or submitting information through a form, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Name

  • Email address
  • Website URL
  • Service interest
  • Business information
  • Optional notes or message details
  • Billing or payment-related information
  • Communication preferences
  • Consent records, including what you agreed to, when you agreed, and the form or page where consent was submitted

We may also collect technical and usage information automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate location
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the website
  • Form interactions
  • Button clicks
  • Scroll activity
  • Session behavior
  • Ad campaign source
  • Call tracking source
  • Cookie identifiers
  • Analytics identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Review your Growth Audit request
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text message about your request
  • Schedule a consultation or Growth Audit
  • Provide services you request
  • Send follow-up information
  • Send marketing or promotional communications, where permitted

  • Measure advertising performance
  • Track lead sources and campaign performance

These tools may collect information about your device, browser, pages visited, actions taken on the website, referral source, ads clicked, forms started or submitted, calls placed, and other website interactions.

We may use advertising platforms such as Google, Meta, or similar providers to show ads, measure ad performance, create custom audiences, retarget website visitors, and understand which marketing campaigns generate leads.

These platforms may use cookies, pixels, device identifiers, or similar technologies to collect or receive information from this website and other websites.

5. Contact Consent

When you check the consent box on our form or submit your contact information, you agree that Hot Call Marketing may contact you using the phone number and email address you provided, including by phone, email, or text message, about your request.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply.

You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any text message, using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contacting us directly.

We may keep records of your consent, including what was agreed to, when it was agreed to, and where the consent was submitted, so we can honor and document your preferences.

6. Email Marketing

If you choose to receive emails from us, we may send service-related emails, follow-up emails, newsletters, promotional messages, or marketing communications.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting us directly.

Even if you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we may still send non-marketing messages related to active services, transactions, scheduled calls, payment issues, or other business communications.

7. Text Messages and Phone Calls

If you provide your phone number and consent to be contacted, we may contact you by phone or text message about your inquiry, requested audit, scheduled call, services, or related follow-up.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary.

You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP. You may also contact us directly to withdraw consent.
